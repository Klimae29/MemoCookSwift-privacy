1. Data collection

When using the app, the following data may be collected:

  • Account information: Authentication is handled via Sign in with Apple. We may receive an email address and a username associated with your Apple account.
  • Anonymous technical identifier: A random Firebase identifier (not linked to your real identity) is generated to authenticate calls to the app's services.
  • User-generated content: Recipes created or imported, and recipe photos you provide for processing.
  • Preferences: App settings and preferences (theme, display options).
  • Crash reports (optional): If the app crashes, an anonymous technical report may be sent to help us fix bugs. You can disable this collection at any time in Settings › Privacy.

2. Use of data

The data collected is used exclusively to:

  • Provide the app's core features
  • Process recipe photos using an artificial intelligence service
  • Enable optional synchronization of user data across devices
  • Diagnose and fix app bugs (crash reports, if enabled)

Photos sent for AI processing are used only for analysis and are not stored by the AI provider beyond processing. No image is reused for any other purpose.

Data is never sold, shared, or transmitted to third parties for commercial purposes.

3. Artificial intelligence (AI) processing

Some app features — recipe import by photo (OCR), import from a web page, YouTube, Instagram or TikTok — rely on artificial intelligence services (LLMs, Large Language Models) to convert raw text into a structured recipe.

Data transmitted to the AI services:

  • What is sent: only the extracted text (OCR content or web page/video text), truncated to a maximum of 10,000 characters.
  • What is never sent: original photos, username, email address, account identifier, or any other personal data.
  • Texts pass through a secure intermediary server (Firebase Cloud Functions) that does not retain any text after processing.

4. Third-party services

The app relies on the following services. Each processes data in accordance with its own privacy policy:

  • Apple services — authentication (Sign in with Apple), synchronization (iCloud / CloudKit) and distribution (App Store). Apple Policy
  • Firebase Authentication (Google) — generates a random anonymous identifier to secure calls to the app's functions. No personally identifiable data is associated with this identifier. Firebase Policy
  • Firebase Analytics (Google) — collects anonymous usage events (e.g., number of recipes created, import source) to improve the app. The Apple advertising identifier (IDFA) is not used. The app does not request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission because no cross-app advertising tracking is performed. Firebase Policy
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) — collects anonymous crash reports to identify and fix bugs. Legal basis: legitimate interest (Art. 6.1.f GDPR) — app stability directly benefits users. You can disable this collection in Settings › Privacy › Crash reports. Firebase Policy
  • Firebase Remote Config (Google) — allows updating some app parameters (quotas, features) without an App Store update. No user data is collected by this service. Firebase Policy

For details on the artificial intelligence services (Mistral AI, Anthropic), see section 3 above.

5. Data storage and security

User data is stored locally on the device by default.

If iCloud synchronization is enabled, data is stored in your personal iCloud space, subject to Apple's privacy policy.

Reasonable security measures are applied to protect user data (encryption of log files, Keychain storage for sensitive credentials, exclusion from iCloud backups for diagnostic files).

6. Data retention period

In accordance with Article 13.2.a of the GDPR, here are the retention periods applied to each category of data:

Data Retention period

User profile (username, optional email) Until the account is deleted

You can request the deletion of all your data at any time from Settings › Privacy › Delete my account.

7. Your rights

In accordance with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the following rights:

  • Right of access (Art. 15) — view and export your data from Settings › Privacy › Export my data
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16) — edit your information directly in the app
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17) — delete your account and all associated data from Settings › Privacy › Delete my account
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20) — export your data in machine-readable JSON format
  • Right to object (Art. 21) — disable crash reports from Settings › Privacy › Crash reports
  • Right to opt out — disable iCloud synchronization at any time in the iOS settings

To exercise your rights or for any question regarding your personal data, contact us at memocook@outlook.fr. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority (in France, the CNIL).

8. Data sharing

MemoCook may share content to other apps (Apple Reminders, text messages, AirDrop) only upon explicit user action. No automatic sharing is performed.
